moipod v0.13.0 goes live on 11 September at 12:00 AM UTC (5:30 AM IST). <!-- slop-lint:ignore -->

What it adds:

Fee payer. An interaction can name an account other than the sender to pay its fuel. That account co-signs and pays; the sender pays nothing for fuel. Apps can now cover fuel for their users.

KMOI on MASN. KMOI moves to MASN, a native asset standard of its own, with a fixed supply: no mint, no burn, including by the manager. KMOI gets a new asset ID. <!-- slop-lint:ignore -->

Pricing in anu. Fuel and storage are priced in anu, the smallest unit of KMOI. 1 KMOI = 1,000,000,000 anu. Minimum fuel price is 50 anu. Storage is 1,000,000 anu per byte.

This is a breaking release. If you build on MOI:

- Update to js-moi-sdk 0.9.0-rc2 and js-polo 0.1.5.
- Stop adding the sender or fee payer to the participant list. The node now reads both from the interaction header.
- Recheck any fixed storage funding values, using the SDK's KMOI and anu conversion functions.
