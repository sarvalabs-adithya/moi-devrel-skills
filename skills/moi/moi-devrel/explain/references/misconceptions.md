# Misconceptions: the wrong pictures people bring, and the correction

Facts are as of 2026-10-03. Each row is a picture someone arrived with, or a draft produced, where the evidence shows it was wrong. The right-hand wording is what to say instead, with its source and date. Wording rules for MOI terms are in `moi-devrel/references/terminology.md`; the claims caught wrong in published or presented text are in `moi-devrel/claim-check/references/caught-claims.md`. `devnet.md` and `repo-map.md` are in `moi-devrel/references/`; `sources-of-truth.md` and `live-reads.md` are in `moi-devrel/claim-check/references/`. A document that quotes a wrong form on purpose carries the lint marker, so the rows below do. "The operator" and `ASK:` are defined in `moi-devrel/SKILL.md`. Back to `../GUIDE.md`.

## How to find the picture someone brings
1. Ask the operator to say the concept in their own words before you teach it (step 2 of `../GUIDE.md`). Listen for account, chain, gas, token, block, contract, and for only, every, nobody, can't.
2. Pictures come from four places: another chain (most often Ethereum, as the docs themselves frame it), early MOI material, a fast draft, and a demo whose staging was read as a protocol fact. Early MOI material that is out of date: Chapter 1 of the first Sprint tutorial, which explained MOI with "Global State Machines" (in the history of `moi-foundation/awesome-moi`, deleted 2026-01-13), and the March 2026 Forge Hacks deck (`sarvalabs-adithya/moi-forge-hacks-demo`).
3. Correct the picture first, then teach. A new fact laid on a wrong picture is remembered wrongly.
