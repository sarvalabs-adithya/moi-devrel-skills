## Where the notes land
- Every calendar event with a Meet makes a `Notes - <event title>` document in the organiser's Drive (about 2.5 KB). A recorded meeting adds the recording, a `Notes by Gemini` document and a `Chat` text file. The notes document has Summary, Decisions, Next steps (assigned by name), Details and the full transcript.
- Calls organised by `info@moi.technology` or `marketing@moinetwork.io` stay in that account and are shared into the operator's Drive. Searching only for files you own misses them. Calls the previous operator organised went to the `Meet Recordings` folder. A Meet started without a calendar event makes a dated folder holding the recording and the notes.
- The notes appear 5 to 90 minutes after the call (the observed spread). Gemini asks the reader to review them, so treat them as drafts.
- Otter transcripts reached the previous operator as pasted text or as `.txt` and `.zip` exports. The ASK for the account owner is in `moi-devrel/references/keys-and-access.md` (Otter.ai, Zoom: owner unknown).
