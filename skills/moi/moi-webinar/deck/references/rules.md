# Rules for every deck

These eleven rules hold for every deck, whoever the audience is. The checker enforces the mechanical ones (badges, backgrounds, em dashes, placeholders). The rest are yours to apply. Back to `../GUIDE.md`. The traps behind several of them are in `traps.md`.

1. **Every number comes from a person or a named source.** Never invent a figure, date, attendance count or outcome. If a slide needs one and nobody supplied it, leave it off and say so on hand-back. Why: community call 14 carried figures from three sources that disagreed and sentences nobody could source, found only after the deck was built.
2. **Photos are real files only.** No stand-in image on a slide about a real event or person. Empty frames that show their filename are fine. Why: for the Bali photos on community call 14 none could be found, so empty frames were built instead of stand-ins.
3. **The corner badge equals the page number.** The cover and the close carry none. Why: badges one behind the pages once got the wrong slide deleted.
4. **Say what the source says.** If the hand-over is vague about who spoke or what happened, the slide stays vague. Flag the gap.
5. **One idea per slide.** Three cards, three stats, four at a push. More is two slides.
6. **Alternate backgrounds** so no two neighbours match (check d).
7. **After deleting or moving a slide, re-check everything:** badges, backgrounds, `TITLES`, `NOTES`, `SCRIPT.md`, words left behind in other slides, the README and the PDF (the list is in `build.md`). Why: community call 14 kept sentences about a campaign slide after it was cut.
8. **Two variant files that have become identical are collapsed to one.** Ask before deleting a file.
9. **Keep `README.md` current with every change:** slide count, flow, photo slots, variants, script (check k reads the count).
10. **Use the approved design exactly.** Start from `templates/template.html`. Do not bring in another design system. Why: for the intro session deck the previous operator rejected a PPTX, then an HTML deck in a different design, and asked for exactly the session 8 look.
11. **Public text rules apply to slides and notes:** no em dashes, no "not this, not that", nothing that goes stale ("last Friday"). The read-aloud rules are in `spoken-script.md`. <!-- slop-lint:ignore -->
